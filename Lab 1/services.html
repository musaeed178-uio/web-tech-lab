Student Services

Support services available to all COMSATS students.

📖

University Library

Access thousands of books, journals, and digital resources. Open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM. Online catalogue available at library.comsats.edu.pk.

💰

Financial Aid & Scholarships

Need-based and merit-based financial assistance. Visit the Financial Aid Office in the Admin Block or email finaid@comsats.edu.pk for eligibility details.

💡

Counselling Centre

Free and confidential mental health support for students. Walk-in sessions available or book an appointment at counselling@comsats.edu.pk.

💼

Career Services

Resume reviews, mock interviews, job placement assistance, and career workshops. Visit career.comsats.edu.pk for upcoming events.

🆔

IT Help Desk

Technical support for university systems, Wi-Fi, email accounts, and software access. Submit a ticket at helpdesk.comsats.edu.pk or visit C-Block Ground Floor.

🏠

Hostel & Accommodation

On-campus hostel facilities for male and female students. Applications open at the start of each semester. Contact hostel@comsats.edu.pk.

🛒

Cafeteria

Multiple food courts serving affordable meals daily. Open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Located on the ground floor of the Main Block.

⚙

Transportation

University shuttle service covers major routes across the city. Bus schedule available at the Transport Office or on the student portal.
