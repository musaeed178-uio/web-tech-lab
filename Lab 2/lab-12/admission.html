Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission criteria?

For BS programs, candidates must have Intermediate (or equivalent) with minimum 50% marks. Admission is based on merit calculated from academic records and entry test.

Is there a scholarship available?

Yes, CUI offers merit-based and need-based scholarships. Top performers can get up to 100% tuition waiver.

Can I transfer from another university?

Transfer admissions are available for students with good academic standing. Credit transfer is subject to departmental approval.

What documents are required?

CNIC/B-Form, Matric certificates, Intermediate transcripts, Equivalence certificate (if applicable), Character certificate, and passport-size photographs.
