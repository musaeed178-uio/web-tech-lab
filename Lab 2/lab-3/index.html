Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus offers comprehensive programs in computer science and related fields.

About the Department

The department was established to provide quality education in computer science and produce skilled professionals for the industry. We have highly qualified faculty and state-of-the-art laboratories.

Programs Offered

BS Computer Science

The BS Computer Science program is a 4-year undergraduate degree that covers fundamentals of computing, programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, and software engineering.

BS Software Engineering

The BS Software Engineering program focuses on the design and development of large-scale software systems. Students learn about software development lifecycle, testing, and project management.
